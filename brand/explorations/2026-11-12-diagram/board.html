Round venn-01: how to label the meeting points

exploration · fixed: circles, colours, centre "Technical Writer + AI"

Question: which way of labelling the three projects is clearer? Recommendation: venn-01/B, because the project sits where it belongs, with no extra marks. The skill words move to the project cards below the diagram.

Baseline · current

Two dots on each meeting point, leader lines to labels outside. Lines cross circle edges.

venn-01/B · Names inside

Only the project name inside its area. No dots, no lines. Skill words ("docs for people and AI") are on the cards, not in the diagram.

venn-01/C · Numbers + legend

A number in each area, a legend under the diagram with the skill and the project. Most information, but the eye must jump to the legend.

venn-01/D · Name + skill inside

Project name and skill words, both inside the area, in two sizes. No dots, no lines. Denser; small text at 12 px.
