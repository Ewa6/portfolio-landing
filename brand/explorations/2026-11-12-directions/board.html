Round directions-01: which creative direction?

exploration · Ewa Jabłońska — technical-writing.net · 2026-11-12 · fixed: name, three-arc idea, blue / purple / aqua family, dark first

Question: which visual idea carries "the writer connects technology, product and users"? Same content in every column. The headline is the current site text (Ewa writes the new one). The arc mark is a draft; the logo has its own round later. Recommendation: A, because it shows the three-area connection without text and keeps the most recognition.

A · Junction

Diagram-led. Each area has one colour: Technology blue, Product purple, Users aqua. Arcs and dots mark where areas meet, on every page.

Trade-off: needs discipline: colour is a code, not decoration.
