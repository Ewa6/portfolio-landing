Date/version: 2026-11-12, v1.1
Status: working. This guide describes the voice Ewa already uses. It is not a source of new copy.
Decision source: D05 — "Ewa writes texts; AI only proofreads", changed by D19 (Mixed rule) and D20 (Oxford comma).

## How to use this guide
Who writes what (D19, "Mixed"):

| Text type | Examples | Who writes | AI's role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative | Hero message, Concept, Process, blog intros, blog posts, case studies | Ewa | Proofreads only: typos, grammar, clarity. AI does not rewrite and does not add content. |
| Functional | Navigation, buttons, card descriptions, meta titles and descriptions, alt text, 404 page, cookie banner | AI drafts | Drafts in this voice. An independent AI review checks the draft. |

- Ewa approves every text before it goes live. She has the last word.
- Ewa can ask AI for a draft of a narrative text. Then AI drafts it, and the copy deck records the exception.
- The copy deck (`copy/copy-deck.md`) records who wrote each text and its status: draft, reviewed, approved.
- Examples below are quotes from Ewa's published texts (source given). There are no AI-written sample lines.

## Voice
Plain, direct, and human. Short sentences. The reader's understanding comes first.

| Trait | In practice | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear | One idea per sentence. The same term for the same thing. | Not cold: speak to the reader as "you". |
| Structured | Headings say what the section does. Steps are in order. | Not bureaucratic: no filler headings, no formal padding. |
| Curious about AI | Say what AI did, what Ewa did, and what Ewa checked. | Not hyped: no "revolution", no "supercharge". |
| Human | First person. Contractions are fine. Honest about what is in progress. | Not casual: no emoji, no jokes that hide the point. |
| In charge | Ewa "sets", "decides", "reviews". Active voice. | Not loud: no superlatives about herself. |

Address/register: first person singular for Ewa ("I"). The reader is "you". English.

## Style
- Brand spelling: "Ewa Jabłońska" with Polish letters, always. Domain in lower case: technical-writing.net.
- Headings and buttons: sentence case. No full stop.
- Punctuation: no emoji. No exclamation marks. No decorative dashes; use a full stop or a colon.
- Lists: use the Oxford comma ("technology, product, and users"). Everywhere in English texts (D20).
- Page titles: "{page} | Ewa Jabłońska" with a vertical bar, not a dash. (Approved in the copy deck, block 1.)
- Numbers and dates: digits; dates as "12 November 2026" in text, ISO in metadata.
- Labels and kickers may use upper case with letter-spacing (max 3–4 words).

## Boundaries
- No claim without proof on the site (a link to a project, a PR, a page).
- No AI-written narrative text presented as Ewa's, unless Ewa asked for the draft and approved it.
- No text goes live without Ewa's approval.
- BrickPrice is not named on the portfolio.

## Proofreading check (for AI)
- Typos, grammar, article use, Oxford comma.
- One term for one meaning (see Vocabulary).
- A sentence that has two ideas: flag it, do not rewrite it.
- Ewa's texts: report findings as a list. Ewa makes the changes.
- AI drafts: the reviewer reports findings; the copywriter changes the draft; Ewa approves.
